Questions before ordering? Send us a message — we will tell you honestly

## FAQ (paste into the listing, and reuse as saved replies)

**Is this a physical product?**
No. This is a digital onboarding and setup service for the Bizora platform.
Access details are sent by email.

**Which businesses does it suit?**
Service businesses that run job by job, made-to-order makers, and any small
business that needs customers, orders, payments and stock in one place.

**Do I have to use every module?**
No — that is the point. You switch on the modules you use and the rest stay out
of the menu.

**Can I use my own words and stages?**
Yes. Item types, workflow stages, custom fields, payment methods and branding
are all configurable per business.

**Do I need new hardware?**
No. Bizora runs in a web browser and on Android phones.

**What do you need from me?**
Your business name, what you sell, the stages a job passes through, the payment
methods you accept, and who needs access.

**Is my data separate from other businesses?**
Yes. Each business has its own workspace with its own members, roles and
records.
