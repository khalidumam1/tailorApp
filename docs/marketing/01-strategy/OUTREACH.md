# Outreach scripts

Direct outreach is where this product will actually sell — owners of workshops,
furniture makers and printers are reachable on WhatsApp and in Facebook groups,
and they answer a specific question far more often than a generic pitch.

Rules for every script here:

1. Ask one question, not for a sale.
2. Never claim a capability that is not in
   [`../FACT_SHEET.md`](../FACT_SHEET.md).
3. Offer the demo, not a discount.

---

## Cold DM — English (Instagram / Facebook)

```
Hi [name] — I saw [their work / post] and had a question, if you have a minute.

When a job is in progress, how do you know today what stage it is at and what
is still owed on it — without asking someone?

Most owners I talk to keep that in three places: a register, a WhatsApp thread
and a diary. We build Bizora for exactly that — one workspace per business with
customers, orders, payments and accounts, set up on a template for your trade.

Happy to show you the same screens configured for [their trade] — 15 minutes,
no obligation. Worth a look?
```

## Email — warm lead

**Subject:** Your [trade] jobs, in one place

```
Hi [name],

Thanks for getting in touch about Bizora.

In plain terms: your business gets its own workspace on Bizora, built from a
[template] template and then configured around how you already work — your
names for things, your stages, your payment methods, and only the modules you
actually use.

Three things owners notice in the first week:
1. Every job has a stage and a history, so status stops living in your head.
2. Payments are recorded against the order, so outstanding is a number, not a
   feeling.
3. Your team can see the same picture, with roles that limit what each person
   can change.

Setup takes three steps, and the first two are ours:
1. You tell us about the business.
2. We configure the workspace.
3. You sign in on web or Android and run it.

When suits for a 15-minute demo? I can show you the screens configured for
[trade] rather than a generic demo.

[Signature]
[Domain] · [Email] · [WhatsApp]
```

## Objection handling

| Objection | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| "My business is different." | That is the point — we do not start from a blank app, we start from a template and then use your item names, your stages and your fields. |
| "We already use WhatsApp." | Keep it. Bizora is where the *record* lives, so status does not depend on scrolling a chat. |
| "My staff will not use software." | They only see the modules you switch on, and each person gets the smallest role that lets them do their job. |
| "What if it does not work out?" | Nothing about how you work changes — you keep your register while you try it. We set it up, so there is no configuration project to abandon. |
| "How much?" | Answer honestly with your real terms, or: "It depends on the modules you need — let me show you the workspace first and quote after." |
| "Can you integrate with [X]?" | No integrations yet — say so. You can export CSV and invoice PDFs. |
| "Do you send WhatsApp updates to customers?" | Notification templates exist; automated delivery is not switched on yet. Be straight about it. |

## What never to say in outreach

- Any customer count, statistic or testimonial — none exist yet.
- "The best / #1 / leading" anything.
- Promises about delivery riders, customer portals or integrations.
- A price that has not been agreed internally.
