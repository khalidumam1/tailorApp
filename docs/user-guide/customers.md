# Customers

## What this is
Your customer book: names, mobile numbers, notes, order history and saved measurements.

## Where to find it
Web: **Customers**. Mobile: **More**, then **Customers** (or search from the home screen).

## Add a customer
1. Choose **Add a customer**.
2. Type the full name and mobile number. Add notes only if the whole team should see them.
3. Save. If the number already exists, the app warns you instead of making a duplicate.

## What happens next
The customer appears in search, can be picked on new orders, and their orders and measurements collect under their name.

## WhatsApp consent
Tick consent only when the customer agrees to WhatsApp messages. If a customer opts out later, use **WhatsApp consent** on their card — messages stop, history stays.

## Archive and restore
Finished with a customer (duplicate, left the area)? Open their card and choose **Archive**. Nothing is deleted — history stays safe. To bring them back: **Show archived** → **Restore**. You are asked to confirm before archiving.

## Export
**Export CSV** downloads your customer list (name, phone, consent, notes) for Excel or backups.

## Common mistakes
- Two spellings of one name create two customers. Search first, then add.
- Writing private remarks in notes — every staff member with customer access can read them.

## Permission warnings
Viewing needs **customers:read**; adding, editing and archiving need **customers:write**.
