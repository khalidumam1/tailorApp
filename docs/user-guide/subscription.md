# Subscription and billing

## What this is
Your shop's own subscription to the platform: current plan, period, invoices, and how to pay for the next period.

## Where to find it
**Subscription & billing** (web sidebar / mobile More).

## Pay or renew
1. Open **Subscription & billing** and check the plan, period end and any grace days left.
2. Choose **Submit payment**: pick the plan and cycle, pay by the shown method, then type the transaction reference exactly as on your bank/mobile receipt.
3. Submit. The payment sits **under review** until the platform team approves it — your period extends only after approval, and you get a receipt PDF.

## If payment is rejected
The reason is shown. Fix the reference or amount and submit again; nothing is double-charged because each submission is reviewed by a person.

## Grace period and limits
After the period ends, a configured grace window keeps things running; after that, sign-in, billing and reading your data still work, but new business writes pause until renewal. Plans can also cap customers, staff or monthly orders — the app tells you when you hit a cap.

## Common mistakes
- Typing the reference from memory. Copy it from the bank app — a wrong reference is the top rejection reason.
- Waiting for the last day. Submit during the trial or period, not after the grace ends.

## Permission warnings
Viewing needs **subscriptions:read**; submitting needs **subscriptions:manage** (usually the owner).
