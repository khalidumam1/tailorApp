# Suppliers and purchasing

## What this is
Who you buy from, what you ordered, what actually arrived, and what you still owe them.

## Where to find it
**Operations & finance**, then the **Suppliers / Purchasing** tabs. Needs the **suppliers** and **purchasing** modules.

## Buy something
1. Add the supplier once (name, phone).
2. Create a purchase order with lines: item, quantity, agreed cost.
3. When goods arrive, **Receive** against the order — type what really arrived, not what was promised.
4. **Settle** when you pay: amount, account, reference. Part payments are fine; the supplier ledger keeps the running balance.

## Common mistakes
- Receiving the full order when half arrived. Short receiving keeps stock and dues honest.
- Paying without a settlement entry. Cash leaves, the ledger still shows a debt.

## Permission warnings
Viewing needs **suppliers:read** / **purchasing:read**; buying and settling need the matching **:write** permissions.
