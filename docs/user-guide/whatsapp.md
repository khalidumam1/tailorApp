# WhatsApp notifications

## What this is
Automatic customer messages — order confirmed, payment received, order ready — plus the delivery history that proves what was sent.

## Where to find it
Web: **WhatsApp notifications**. Mobile: **More**, then **Notifications**.

## How it works
1. The platform team connects one sender number and approves message templates with Meta.
2. When an order is created, a payment recorded or an order becomes ready, the app queues the matching message for customers who consented.
3. The history shows each message: queued, sent, delivered, or failed with the reason.

## Consent matters
Messages only go to customers who agreed (see *Customers*). If someone replies STOP or asks you directly, remove consent on their customer card — no new messages queue for them.

## Common mistakes
- Assuming a queued message was delivered. Check history; a failed message shows why (wrong number, template issue).
- Editing template wording casually. Templates are approved text — changes need platform approval again.

## Permission warnings
History needs **notifications:read**. Template settings are owner-level (**settings:manage**) plus platform approval.
