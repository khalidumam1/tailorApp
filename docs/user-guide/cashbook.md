# Cashbook — daily cash and bank

## What this is
The day book for real money: cash in the drawer, bank balance, expenses and transfers between accounts.

## Where to find it
**Operations & finance**, then the **Cashbook** tab. Needs the **cashbook** module.

## Record money movement
1. Pick the account (Cash, Bank…).
2. Choose the kind: **Receipt** (money in), **Expense** (money out) or **Transfer** (between your own accounts).
3. Type amount, date, reference and a memo your future self will understand ("Electricity bill March", not "bill").
4. Save. Each entry also posts the matching accounting journal automatically.

## Reconciliation
When the bank statement arrives, compare statement lines with cashbook entries for the period; differences are missing entries or bank charges — record them, never force the total.

## Common mistakes
- Using the cashbook for customer payments. Those belong on the order (**Record payment**) so the customer's balance stays right; the cashbook then receives its entry automatically through accounting setup.
- Back-dating without a memo. Dates can be corrected, but write why.

## Permission warnings
Viewing needs **cashbook:read**; entries need **cashbook:write**. Many owners keep entry rights with one trusted person.
