# Accounting reports

## What this is
The formal books behind the shop: journals, trial balance, profit & loss and balance sheet, fed automatically by orders, payments, purchasing and the cashbook.

## Where to find it
**Operations & finance**, then the **Accounting** tab. Needs the **accounting** module.

## Read the reports
- **Trial balance** — every account's debit/credit totals; it must balance. If it doesn't, stop and find the journal causing it.
- **Profit & loss** — income minus expenses for a chosen period. Pick dates in your business timezone; totals follow those local days.
- **Balance sheet** — what the business owns and owes on a chosen date.
- **Journals** — the double-entry story behind every transaction; open one to see exactly which accounts moved.

## Exports
Use **Export CSV** on the Customers/Orders/Payments screens and **Ledger CSV** on the Payments page for accountant-friendly files.

## Common mistakes
- Judging profit from the till. Cash in the drawer ignores dues, stock and unpaid supplier bills — profit & loss does not.
- Picking report dates in a different timezone mindset. The app uses your business timezone for day boundaries.

## Permission warnings
Viewing needs **accounting:read**. Account setup changes need **accounting:manage** and should be rare and deliberate.
