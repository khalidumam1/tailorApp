# Quick Start — your first day

## What this is
The shortest path from a new account to your first finished order. Follow it once and you know the whole shop flow.

## Before you start
- Your business owner or the platform team has created your account.
- You have your email and a password. If an administrator reset your password, the app will ask you to choose your own new password first — this is normal.

## Steps
1. Open the app (web address from your owner, or the mobile app) and sign in with your email and password.
2. If you work in more than one business, pick your business from the list. Platform staff pick **Platform** instead.
3. The **Overview** shows today's work: new orders, work due today, overdue orders, money still to collect, and open alterations.
4. Add your first customer (Customers, then Add a customer). Type their name and mobile number.
5. Create your first order (Orders → New order): pick the customer, the item, quantity, price and the promised date, then save.
6. When the customer pays any amount, open the order and choose **Record payment**. The order's outstanding balance goes down immediately.
7. Move the order through your workshop stages with the **Move to …** buttons until it is ready and collected.

## What happens next
Every order, payment and stage change is saved on the server at once, written into the audit history, and (if WhatsApp is connected) queued as a customer message.

## Common mistakes
- Typing the customer number without the country code is fine — the app stores it in international format for WhatsApp automatically.
- Choosing the wrong business at sign-in. Check the business name at the top before entering data.

## Permission warnings
You only see the sections your role allows. If a button is missing, ask your business owner — it is a permission, not a fault.
