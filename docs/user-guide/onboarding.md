# Setting up your business (owner checklist)

## What this is
The one-time setup that makes the app speak your shop's language: your item names, your workflow stages, your receipt branding.

## Where to find it
Web: **Business settings**. Mobile: **More**, then the Business settings item.

## Steps
1. **Name and logo** — set the name customers should see on receipts and invoices. Pick a logo style and colours, or paste a logo URL.
2. **Modules** — switch on only what you use (Catalog, Inventory, Purchasing, Cashbook, Accounting…). Hidden modules disappear from everyone's menu.
3. **Item types** — add the things you sell (for example Shalwar Kameez, Suit, Alteration). Orders and the catalog use these names everywhere.
4. **Workflow stages** — check the stages an order passes through (for example Cutting → Stitching → Fitting → Ready). The first stage is where new orders start.
5. **Payment methods** — choose which methods you accept (Cash, Bank, Digital).
6. **Custom fields** — add extra boxes you always need (for example fabric type on an order, or neck size on a customer).
7. Invite your team from **Team** (see *Staff and roles*) and give each person the smallest role that lets them do their job.

## What you type
Names exactly as customers should read them — they appear on receipts and WhatsApp messages.

## What happens next
Settings apply immediately for everyone. Workflow or field changes only affect new screens; old orders keep the history they were created with.

## Common mistakes
- Turning on every module at once. Start with Customers, Orders and Payments; add ERP modules when you need them.
- Renaming stages after orders exist. Old orders keep their stage history; new orders use the new stages.

## Permission warnings
Only accounts with **settings:manage** can change setup. Owners should keep at least one trusted second admin.
