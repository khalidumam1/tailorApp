# Payments, receipts and exports

## What this is
Money in: advances and balances, printed receipts, corrections done properly, and exports for your records.

## Record a payment
1. Open the order and choose **Record payment**.
2. Type the amount exactly as received and the method (Cash, Bank, Digital).
3. Save. The receipt number is assigned automatically; the order's outstanding balance drops at once.

## Receipts
Every payment can be re-printed or sent: **Receipt PDF** downloads it; on mobile you can share it straight to the customer's WhatsApp.

## Corrections — read this twice
A posted payment is never edited and never deleted. Typed 5,000 instead of 500? Use **Reverse** on that payment with a reason, then record the correct payment. Both entries stay visible — that is what makes the ledger trustworthy.

## Invoice for an order
**Invoice PDF** (order card) shows all items, the order total, every payment and the balance due. **Export CSV** buttons on Customers, Orders and Payments download lists for Excel; **Ledger CSV** (Payments page) exports cashbook entries. Very long exports are capped and the file says so — narrow by screen first if that happens.

## Common mistakes
- Taking a payment on the wrong order. Check the order number on the receipt before handing it over.
- Recording tomorrow's promised payment today. Only record money actually received.

## Permission warnings
Recording and reversing need **payments:write**; viewing and receipts need **payments:read**; ledger export needs **cashbook:read**. Reversals should be a senior person's job.

## Roman Urdu mein
