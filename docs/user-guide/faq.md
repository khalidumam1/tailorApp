# FAQ and troubleshooting

## I forgot my password. Where is the reset link?
There isn't one — by design. Ask your business owner (or, for owners, the platform team) to use **Reset password** in Team settings. You will get a temporary password, sign in, and immediately choose your own. See *Staff, roles and passwords*.

## A button or whole section is missing for me.
Two usual causes: your **role** lacks the permission, or the owner switched the **module** off. Both are fixed by the owner in settings — not by reinstalling anything.

## The app says I am offline / read-only.
Check the device connection. The web workspace turns read-only offline so nobody creates conflicting records; mobile keeps showing your downloaded data. Try again when the connection returns — nothing you saved online is lost.

## I saved an order and the connection dropped. Is it duplicated?
No. Order saving uses a one-time save key: retrying the same save returns the original order instead of a twin. Check the Orders list before creating another.

## A payment was typed wrong.
Never look for an edit button — there is none, on purpose. **Reverse** the payment with a reason, then record the correct one. Both show in history. See *Payments, receipts and exports*.

## The customer says they never got the WhatsApp.
Open **WhatsApp notifications** history: queued/sent/failed is shown with the reason. Also confirm the customer consented and the number (with country code) is right.

## Why did writes stop with a subscription message?
Your plan period and grace days ended, or a plan cap (customers/staff/monthly orders) was reached. The owner renews under **Subscription & billing**; after platform approval, writes resume.

## Numbers look wrong on the dashboard today.
Dashboards use your business timezone for "today". If the shop's timezone setting is wrong, days shift — owners fix it in Business settings. Money totals always come from posted payments, never from typing.

## We archived the wrong customer/order/business.
Nothing is lost. **Show archived** → **Restore** (businesses: the platform team's Archived filter). History and payments return exactly as they were.

## Who changed this record?
Ask an owner to check the business **audit history** (platform team: platform Audit history). Every privileged change records who, when and what.
