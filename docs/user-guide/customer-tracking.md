# Customer tracking link

## What this is
A private link you send to the customer for **one** order. It opens a small page that shows where the order is, what it costs, what is still due and when it will be ready — no account, no app, no password. The customer can also confirm their details or ask you a question, and it lands on the order in your workspace.

It is meant to replace the "bhaiya, order ready hua?" phone call.

## Where to find it
Web and mobile: open the order, then press **Customer link**.

## Create and share the link
1. Open the order and press **Customer link**.
2. Press **Create tracking link**. The link is created and copied to your clipboard.
3. Press **Send on WhatsApp** to open WhatsApp with the customer's number and the message ready, or **Copy link** to paste it anywhere else.

The link is one link per order. Pressing **Create tracking link** again keeps the same link, so the copy the customer already has never stops working.

## What the customer sees
- The shop name and a greeting with their first name.
- The current stage of the work and the date it is promised by.
- The items, the total, what they have paid and the balance still due.
- Their own measurements, exactly as they were recorded.
- The progress timeline: every stage the order has passed through.

They never see your internal notes, your costs, other customers or any other order.

## Customer messages
On the order card, under **Customer link**, the **Customer messages** list shows what the customer sent:

- **My details are correct** — the customer confirmed their measurements or details.
- **I have a question** — the customer asked something; press **Mark answered** once you have replied.
- **I want to request a change** — the customer asked for a change to the work.

Press **Mark answered** to close a message, or **Reopen** to bring it back. Answering on WhatsApp and then marking it answered keeps the order tidy.

## Revoke or replace the link
- **Revoke link** closes the page immediately. Use it when the customer should no longer have access, or when the link was sent to the wrong person.
- After revoking, press **Create tracking link** again to issue a new link with a new address; the old one stays dead.

## Common mistakes
- Sending the link to a WhatsApp group instead of the customer — anyone in the group can open it.
- Revoking a link because the customer opened it several times. Opening is counted and shown to you; it is not a problem.
- Forgetting to mark a message as answered, so the list of open messages grows.

## Permission warnings
Viewing the link and reading customer messages needs **orders:read**. Creating, rotating and revoking links needs **orders:share**. Marking a message answered needs **orders:write**.
