# Business settings and branding

## What this is
The control room for how your workspace looks and behaves: name, logo, modules, fields, workflow stages, dashboard widgets and payment methods.

## Where to find it
Web: **Business settings**. Mobile: **More**, then the Business settings item.

## What you can change
- **Branding** — business name, logo style/colours or logo URL. Receipts, invoices and sign-in show these.
- **Modules** — switch features on/off for the whole business.
- **Item types and custom fields** — see the owner checklist in *Setting up your business*.
- **Workflow stages** — the steps your orders travel through.
- **Dashboard widgets** — which counters the Overview shows.
- **Payment methods** — Cash, Bank, Digital (methods customers can use with you).

## Save carefully
Saving publishes immediately for the whole team and is written to the configuration history, so an earlier version can be reviewed. Change one thing at a time until you learn the effects.

## Common mistakes
- Switching off a module your team still uses daily. Their menu loses it at once (data is safe and returns when you switch back on).
- A logo URL that needs a login to view. Use a public image address or the built-in logo styles.

## Permission warnings
Everything here needs **settings:manage**. Keep this permission with owners and one trusted deputy.
