# Staff, roles and passwords

## What this is
Who can sign in to your business, what each person is allowed to do, and what to do when someone forgets a password.

## Where to find it
Web: **Business settings**, then **Team**. Mobile: **More**, then Business settings, then Team.

## Add a team member
1. Choose **Create member**, type their name, email and a temporary password (at least 16 characters).
2. Pick a role. Create roles first if none fit: **Create role**, name it for the job (Counter, Tailor, Manager) and tick only the permissions that job needs.
3. Share the temporary password privately (in person is best). At their first sign-in the app makes them choose their own password.

## Forgot password? (very common)
There is deliberately no "forgot password" email — passwords are reset by people, not links:
1. Owner (or a **staff:manage** holder) opens **Team**, finds the member, chooses **Reset password**.
2. Type a new temporary password and confirm. The member is signed out everywhere at once.
3. They sign in with the temporary password and must choose their own immediately.

You can also change **your own** password any time: web account menu → **Change password**; mobile **Settings**, then the **Password** section.

## Removing access
- **Deactivate or remove member** signs them out and blocks the business for them; their past work stays under their name. Removing asks for confirmation.
- The business owner account cannot be removed, and you cannot remove or reset yourself — another admin must do it.

## Common mistakes
- Giving everyone the owner role "to keep it simple". One shared all-powerful account means one leaked password owns the shop.
- Writing temporary passwords in the shop WhatsApp group. Hand them over privately and let the forced change do its work.

## Permission warnings
Team management needs **staff:manage**. Role permissions can never exceed your own — the app blocks granting what you do not hold.
