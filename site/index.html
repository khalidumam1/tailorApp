Questions

Before you start

What happens when the trial ends?

Nothing disappears. If you do not subscribe, the workspace becomes read-only: you can still sign in, read your records and export them, and subscribe at any point to start writing again.

Do you offer a discount?

No. The price is the same for everyone, which is why we can keep it low and answer support in hours rather than days. The one exception is the 50% rate for registered NGOs and educational institutions.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Reports export to CSV, and you can ask us for a full export of your records at any time. It is your data.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Bizora runs in a browser. Staff who prefer a phone can install the Android app and use the same account.

Can my staff use it at the same time as me?

Yes. Every member gets a sign-in, and you choose what each role can see and change.

Which trade is it for?

Six templates ship today: Tailor, Furniture, Carpenter, Auto Workshop, Printing and Generic. Each one sets the words, stages and fields for that trade. Other business types are configured on the platform.

How do I cancel?

Write to us and we will close the account and confirm it in writing.

How do I reach a human?

Email us. We answer within one working day. Support is written and asynchronous — we do not run a phone line, and that is part of how the price stays low.
